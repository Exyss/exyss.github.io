I am a recent graduate of the Master's degree program in Computer Science at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://www.uniroma1.it/it/), where I had the honor of being advised by [Nicola Galesi](https://sites.google.com/diag.uniroma1.it/nicolagalesi) and [Massimo Lauria](https://www.massimolauria.net/).

My research focuses on computational complexity theory, logic and combinatorics. In particular, I am very interested in investigating ways in which proof complexity can be used as a lens for deriving results about impossibility of efficient computation, such as meta-complexity and TFNP. Currently, I work as a research intern at Sapienza under the supervision of [Tiziana Calamoneri](https://calamo.di.uniroma1.it/), where I study algorithms for solving computational problems on graphs.

In 2025, I received the 3+2 Award from the [Italian Association for Logics and its Applications (AILA)](https://www.ailalogica.it/) for Best Bachelor Thesis in Logics. In the same year, I was recipient of the BOOST'25 Summer School Scholarship, issued by [Fondazione Elicsir](https://www.elicsir.it/), and the 2025 Logic Summer School Scholarship, issued by AILA. From 2023 to 2026, I have been appointed as main teaching assistant for the 1st and 2nd year courses "Introduction to Algorithms" and "Algorithm Design" of the Bachelor degree in Computer Science at Sapienza University of Rome.

You can find my full CV [here](/assets/pdf/CV_Simone_Bianco.pdf).
